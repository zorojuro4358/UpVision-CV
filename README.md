# UpVision

UpVision is an AI-powered monitoring and analytics system for the educational process driven by Computer Vision. The project is designed for schools and educational centers to automatically analyze student engagement, attendance, and behavior during classes.

## What is it?
UpVision is an EdTech platform that transforms standard classroom video feeds into structured, actionable data. Utilizing advanced neural networks, the system helps educators and administrators evaluate lesson effectiveness, identify gaps in student concentration, and automate routine tasks.

## What does it do?
- **Tracks Engagement (Attention Tracking):** Analyzes posture, gestures, and micro-expressions to determine the focus level of both individual students and the classroom as a whole.
- **Automates Attendance:** Logs student presence in real time via facial recognition, eliminating the need for manual roll calls.
- **Detects Behavioral Anomalies:** Flags signs of fatigue, phone distractions, or side conversations.
- **Generates Analytical Reports:** Provides teachers and parents with comprehensive dashboards tracking a child's focus dynamics across different subjects.

## How does it work?
1. **Video Capture:** Standard classroom cameras stream live video feeds to the processing server in real time.
2. **AI Analysis:** Computer vision algorithms process the frames, detecting individuals, recognizing faces, and classifying actions.
3. **Aggregation & Metrics:** The processed data is converted into an "Attention Score" for each segment of the lesson.
4. **Feedback:** Results are visualized on a web interface for school administration and delivered as reports or notifications to teachers and parents.
